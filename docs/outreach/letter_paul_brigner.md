**Subject:** Pedalshield — a live shielded-payout app, and where ZODL's expertise would be decisive

Dear Paul,

I'm Sam Newman, founder of IntelliGrip Industries. I'm building **Pedalshield** — *ride private, earn shielded* — a privacy-first bike-to-earn app on Zcash, and I'm writing because the next stretch needs exactly the expertise and ecosystem support you and ZODL bring. I'd be grateful for 15 minutes of your time.

Briefly, what's already real — not a pitch deck, a running system:

- A rider tracks a real ride; the phone verifies it **entirely on-device** — the GPS route never leaves the phone, enforced by a unit test, not a privacy policy.
- An **autonomous backend pays real shielded ZEC on mainnet**, with no human in the loop — reproducible, with verifiable on-chain txids (e.g. `a64f2b15…2db2f1d8`). The deployed backend is live and secured.
- It's open source (MIT) and our ZecHub Hackathon 2026 submission (Games track).

Why I believe this matters to Zcash, and to ZODL's mission specifically: **Pedalshield is a demand engine.** Every rider gets a shielded address and a recurring, real-world reason to receive private payments. That is onboarding ordinary, non-crypto people into *holding and using* shielded ZEC — the adoption the protocol most needs and can least manufacture on its own.

Three places your support would be decisive:

1. **Policy and regulatory guidance — your domain.** Paying ZEC rewards for real-world activity raises real questions: money-transmission posture by jurisdiction, app-store crypto-reward rules, KYC thresholds. I want to build this correctly from the start, and I don't know a better person to help me navigate it.
2. **A bridge to ZODL's wallet engineering.** The one piece between us and riders truly "earning shielded" is a real on-device wallet — the same ZcashLightClientKit foundation the Zodl wallet is built on. A short conversation with your team could save us months.
3. **Ecosystem support and a grants path.** As an open-source public good that drives shielded adoption, Pedalshield sits squarely in the Zcash Community Grants wheelhouse, and I'd value your perspective.

I'll be honest about the limits, because that's the ethos: payouts are small and capped, the rider wallet is still being made real, and ZK route proofs are roadmap. Privacy is the product, not yield.

Could I have 15 minutes — a call, or a few async notes — in the next couple of weeks? I can send a 90-second demo (real ride → on-device verification → shielded payout → txid) ahead of time. Pedalshield is live at **pedalshield.app**, and the code is at **github.com/intelligrip/Pedalshield**.

Thank you for everything you do for Zcash. I'd be grateful for your guidance.

Warmly,
