# Pedalshield — Demo Video Screenplay (ZecHub Hackathon 3.0)

**Goal:** In ~90 seconds, prove the whole loop is real — *ride → verified on-device → real shielded ZEC on mainnet* — and make privacy the hero.
**Target length:** 75–90s (a 30s teaser cut is at the bottom).
**Tone:** calm, confident, honest. No hype. Let the real txid do the bragging.
**Where it goes:** unlisted YouTube/Vimeo link → into the submission README `Demo video:` line → posted in Zcash Global Discord **#zechub**.

---

## The golden rule

The **txid** and the **block-explorer** shots must be genuine screen recordings of the real system. That authenticity is the submission. You may use stock/AI b-roll for the cycling transitions, but never fake the app, the payout, or the explorer.

---

## Positioning: the Zcash growth story (say it accurately)

The big idea to land: **Pedalshield is a demand / adoption engine for Zcash.** Every verified ride creates a real shielded transaction from an ordinary, non-crypto person — manufacturing the genuine, recurring private-payment demand the network needs and can't generate on its own.

Say "adoption engine," "demand engine," or "growth engine for shielded usage." **Avoid "scalability"** — to this audience that means protocol throughput (TPS / proving), which Pedalshield doesn't improve (it adds load). The adoption framing is just as powerful and fully defensible. Anchor line: *"Mining funds riding; riding funds Zcash."*

## Shot inventory — what to capture first

Record these clips, then assemble. (iPhone: Settings → Control Center → add **Screen Recording**; long-press the record button to confirm mic if you want live audio.)

1. **A real ride in the app** — screen-record from your phone: tap Start → "Acquiring GPS… → GPS ±Xm" → distance ticking up, privacy padlock "On device only" visible. 10–15s is plenty.
2. **Finishing the ride** — the hold-to-finish, then the integrity-score reveal animation + "verified."
3. **The payout pipeline** — the stages running, ending on a **real txid**.
4. **The block explorer** — open `mainnet.zcashexplorer.app/transactions/<that txid>` in a browser; show it's a real shielded tx on mainnet.
5. **(Optional) real-world b-roll** — 3–5s of you actually riding, for the open/close.
6. **(Optional) the website** — pedalshield.app hero for the closing card.

## Clean voiceover script (read straight through ≈ 90s)

> Your cycling app records every route you ride — where you live, where you sleep, when you're not home.
>
> Pedalshield is the opposite. You get paid to ride — and your route never leaves your phone.
>
> I start a ride. GPS and motion are tracked right here on the device. Only an anonymous distance claim ever leaves — never the map.
>
> When I finish, the phone scores the ride for cheating — cars, walks, spoofs — and verifies it. A unit test guarantees no GPS point can leak.
>
> Then an autonomous treasury pays real shielded ZEC on Zcash mainnet. No operator approves it. No one watches it.
>
> Here's that exact transaction on the block explorer — a real shielded payout, on mainnet, that you can verify yourself.
>
> It's non-custodial. The ZEC lands in a Zcash wallet you already control. We never hold your keys or your money.
>
> And here's why it matters: every paid ride is a new shielded transaction from a real person. Pedalshield turns everyday cyclists into everyday users of private money — that's the adoption Zcash needs most. Mining funds riding; riding funds Zcash.
>
> Ride private. Earn shielded. Pedalshield — open source, built on Zcash.

## Production tips

- **Keep it real where it counts.** The app flow, the txid, and the explorer must be genuine. That's the whole point versus a slideshow.
- **Voice:** record yourself, or paste the clean script into an AI voice (ElevenLabs / similar) for a steady read.
- **Music:** low, minimal, no copyright. Calm dawn-ride feel matches the brand.
- **Text legibility:** big, high-contrast captions — most people watch muted first.
- **Length:** under 2 minutes is the rule; ~90s is the sweet spot. Don't pad.
- **Export:** 1080p, upload unlisted, then paste the link into the submission README and the Discord post.
