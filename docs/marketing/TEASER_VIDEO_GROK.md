# Pedalshield — Launch Teaser Video (Grok Imagine plan)

_~20–30s hybrid teaser for X / Reddit / Instagram / TikTok / the website. Honest-claims rule applies: do NOT fake the app working or imply earnings. AI b-roll sets the mood; **real** clips carry the proof._

## What Grok Imagine can / can't do
- ✅ **Can:** cinematic b-roll — cyclists, light, privacy/shield motifs, premium hardware-style shots, abstract "shielded value" visuals.
- ❌ **Can't:** show your actual app UI, a real ride, or a real payout. Don't try to make it — those must be **real screen captures**, or you're overclaiming.

## The winning structure (hybrid, ~25s, vertical 9:16)
1. **0–4s — Hook (AI b-roll):** cyclist at golden hour. Caption: *"Your fitness app sells the map of your life."*
2. **4–8s — Problem (AI abstract):** a glowing route dissolving. Caption: *"Pedalshield never uploads your route."*
3. **8–14s — Proof (REAL screen capture):** 3–5s of the actual app — ride tracking + the payout card. Caption: *"Verified on your phone. Paid in shielded ZEC."*
4. **14–18s — Proof² (REAL):** the real txid in the block explorer. Caption: *"Real. On Zcash mainnet."*
5. **18–22s — Product hint (AI hardware shot):** the bar-end edge node, premium. Caption: *"Optional edge node. Always-on. Trustless."*
6. **22–25s — End card (edit, not AI):** shield icon + **"Ride private. Earn shielded."** + **pedalshield.app** + "Built on Zcash · Open source."

> Keep captions on (most people watch muted). Keep earnings language honest — no "cash per mile," no numbers implying riches.
