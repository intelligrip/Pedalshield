# Pedalshield — Competitive Analysis (move-to-earn + fitness)

_Honest read of the field as of mid-2026. The goal isn't to out-promise the category — it's to be the antidote to its sins. Sources at bottom._

## The landscape

| App | Activity | Pays in | Buy-in | Privacy | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **STEPN** | walk/run | GST/GMT token | **NFT sneakers ($$$)** | data harvested | survived bear market; "fitness-first w/ incentives"; token volatile, rule changes hurt small earners |
| **Sweatcoin / SWEAT** | walk/run/cycle/swim | SWEAT token (NEAR) | free | data harvested | huge install base; **pays fractions of a cent/day** |
| **DEFIT** | multi (incl. cycling) | token | varies | data harvested | smaller multi-activity M2E |
| **Strava** (incumbent) | all | nothing (subscription) | free | **sells/exposes data (heatmaps)** | the category leader for tracking + social |
| **Pedalshield** | cycling | **real shielded ZEC** | free, non-custodial | **route never leaves phone** | live mainnet payouts, pre-launch |

## What the category does WELL (we must match)

- **Frictionless onboarding (Sweatcoin).** Download → connect health app → earn. Zero crypto knowledge. This drove a massive install base. *Our "paste a Unified Address" is a wall by comparison.*
- **Distribution + virality.** Referral loops, social sharing, App-Store presence on iOS *and* Android. *We have none of this yet, and we're iOS-only.*
- **Engagement depth.** STEPN's sneaker leveling/progression kept people hooked. *Our streaks + leaderboard are thin.*
- **Perceived earnings at entry.** STEPN can show $1–5/day (after the NFT cost). *Our carbon-rate payout is dust per ride.*

## What NONE of them do (our wedge — lean in hard)

- **Privacy.** Every competitor harvests your data / uploads your route. **Not one is private.** Our on-device verification (route never leaves the phone, unit-tested) + non-custodial design is genuinely unique. No incumbent can copy this without rebuilding their whole data-monetized model.
- **Real, non-speculative money.** They pay in *their own token* (SWEAT, GST/GMT) — speculative, frequently collapsed, or literally pennies. We pay **real shielded ZEC**, an established asset, not a project token.
- **No buy-in, no NFT gate.** STEPN makes you buy sneakers to earn. We're free to start.
- **Honest, sustainable economics.** The category's defining failure is ponzi-ish tokenomics — token crashes, rule changes that punish small earners, "marketing vs. real payout" gaps. Our **no-token, carbon-pegged, honest-limits** model is a *trust* differentiator built from their failures.
- **Carbon/real-world value.** None tie rewards to a real externality. We pay the value of the CO2 you avoided — meaningful and a story.

## Where WE must improve (steal from them)

1. **Kill onboarding friction — #1.** Make the crypto invisible. Progressive onboarding (ride first, connect wallet later), an optional in-app wallet so "paste a Unified Address" isn't the first wall. This is the single biggest gap vs. Sweatcoin.
2. **Make rewards *feel* worth it.** The carbon rate is honest but tiny per ride. Accrual (done) + redemption/partner discounts give the dust a destination and real purchasing power.
3. **Build virality.** Referral/invite mechanics, the QR funnel, community presence. Growth is a feature we haven't built.
4. **Android + reliable background tracking.** Competitors have both; we're iOS-only and tracking dies on screen-off. Table stakes for real riders.
5. **Deepen engagement.** Progression beyond streaks — goals, challenges, the integrity-score "game," verifiable badges.

## The positioning takeaway

Do **not** compete on "earn the most" — that's a race to over-promise and collapse, and the whole category has a trust hangover from exactly that. Compete as **the honest, private, real-money antidote**:

> "Move-to-earn promised you riches, harvested your data, paid you a worthless token, and made you buy an NFT first. Pedalshield pays you real private money for a real thing you did — and never touches your data. No token. No buy-in. No surveillance."

The category's sins (data harvesting, token ponzinomics, NFT gates, pennies-dressed-as-riches) are our marketing. We win the trust-fatigued user the incumbents can't keep — not by earning more, but by being the one they can believe.
