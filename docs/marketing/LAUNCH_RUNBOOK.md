## What to watch (your launch dashboard)
- **Waitlist signups** (Formspree) — the headline number.
- **Site visits + click-through** to the waitlist (site analytics).
- **Forum / Discord engagement** — replies, sentiment, hard questions.
- **X impressions + profile clicks**; **Reddit upvotes/comments**.
- **Conversion**: visits → signups. If traffic is high but signups low, fix the hero/CTA, not the channels.
