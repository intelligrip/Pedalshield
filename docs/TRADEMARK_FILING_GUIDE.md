# PEDALSHIELD — USPTO trademark filing guide

Prepared July 5, 2026. Owner: your LLC. Scope: classes 9, 36, 42.
Estimated government fees: **$1,050 now** (3 × $350), **$450 later**
(3 × $150 Statement of Use when the app launches publicly).

> Not legal advice. This is a well-prepared DIY filing; a 30-minute review
> by a trademark attorney before submitting is cheap insurance if anything
> below feels uncertain.

---

## Step 0 — before you file (15 minutes)

**A. Confirm the LLC details.** The application must state the owner's
exact legal name and state of formation, e.g. `Intelligrip Industries LLC,
an Oregon limited liability company`. If the LLC isn't formed yet, form it
first (Oregon: sos.oregon.gov, $100) — or file as an individual and assign
to the LLC later.

**B. Knockout search.** Go to https://tmsearch.uspto.gov and search:

- `pedalshield` — exact
- `pedal shield` — two words
- `pedalshield` with "Sounds like" / plural variants

For each hit, check the class and goods. A guitar-electronics
"pedalSHIELD" (ElectroSmash) is a different market — generally not a
conflict — but a live mark in classes 9, 36, or 42 for anything
software/crypto/fitness IS a problem: stop and get an attorney opinion
before spending $1,050.

**C. Create a USPTO.gov account** (myuspto) and verify identity (ID.me) —
required before TEAS lets you file.

## Step 1 — the application (TEAS, ~30 minutes)

File at https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/apply → "Apply online" →
Trademark/Base application.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| Mark type | **Standard characters** |
| The mark | `PEDALSHIELD` |
| Owner | Your LLC's exact legal name, entity type "limited liability company", state of formation, business address |
| Filing basis | **Section 1(b) — Intent to use** for all three classes (see note below) |
| Attorney | None (you may self-represent as a US-domiciled owner) |

**Why 1(b) intent-to-use:** the app is in TestFlight beta. A free beta is a
gray area for "use in commerce," and a refused specimen wastes months.
1(b) locks in your priority date today; you file the Statement of Use
($150/class) once Pedalshield is on the public App Store — you have 6
months after the Notice of Allowance, extendable ($125/class per 6-month
extension, up to 5).

## Step 2 — goods & services (the part that matters)

Use the **ID Manual picker inside TEAS** (search the terms below and
select the pre-approved entry, filling in the blanks). Free-form text
costs +$200/class and invites rejection — don't use it.

**Class 9** — search "downloadable mobile application":

> Downloadable mobile applications for *tracking and verifying bicycle
> rides and for receiving, storing and transferring cryptocurrency*

**Class 36** — search "cryptocurrency" and pick the closest entries; two
good candidates (you can list more than one within a class at no extra
cost):

> Financial services, namely, providing electronic transfer of a virtual
> currency for use by members of an online community via a global computer
> network
>
> Cryptocurrency payment processing

**Class 42** — search "software as a service":

> Software as a service (SAAS) services featuring software for *verifying
> physical activity and bicycle ride data*

The exact pre-approved wording in the picker may differ slightly — pick
the nearest entry and fill blanks with the italicized language. If nothing
close exists in a class, pause rather than free-typing.

## Step 3 — pay and calendar

- Pay **$1,050** (3 classes × $350).
- Save the serial number. Track status at https://tsdr.uspto.gov.
- Calendar these:
  - **~3–4 months:** an examining attorney reviews it. Respond to any
    Office Action within 3 months (or pay a $100 extension).
  - **After approval:** published for 30-day opposition, then Notice of
    Allowance.
  - **Within 6 months of the Notice of Allowance:** file Statement of Use
    ($150/class) with specimens — screenshots of the public App Store
    listing showing PEDALSHIELD (class 9), the in-app payout screen /
    website showing the ZEC reward service under the mark (36), and the
    verification service described on the website (42).

## While it's pending

- Use **™** next to Pedalshield on the site and app now; switch to **®**
  only after registration.
- Registration will take roughly 12–18 months end to end. Rights date
  back to today's filing.
- After registration: maintenance filing between years 5–6 (Section 8,
  $325/class), then every 10 years.
