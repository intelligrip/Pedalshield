Shielded payments on Zcash · Bend, Oregon

Proof of payment, never proof of whereabouts.

Employers and cities pay people to bike to work, and to prove the rides they collect GPS trails. Pedalshield verifies each ride on the phone and pays riders in shielded ZEC on mainnet. Ghost Commute gives the sponsor an audit that proves every payout on chain and contains no route.

The problem

Proof means surveillance. A sponsor that can't see the rides asks for a GPS trail: home, office and schedule, sitting in a vendor's database. Washington, Oregon and California now treat that data as sensitive or health data.

Public payouts are worse. Pay riders on a transparent blockchain and "home to work, every weekday" becomes a permanent public record. The payout history is the map.

How it works

  1. 1

    Verified on the phone

    The Pedalshield app checks the ride on the device. The claim it sends has no coordinates; an open-source test enforces that.

    Live
  2. 2

    Paid in shielded ZEC

    An autonomous treasury, built on librustzcash, pays the rider's own wallet on Zcash mainnet. Sender, recipient and amount are hidden. Ironwood pool since NU6.3.

    Live
  3. 3

    Audited without the route

    Ghost Commute uses the treasury's viewing key to issue a one-note disclosure per payout. Anyone can check the amount and recipient against the chain. The route was never sent anywhere.

    Live on mainnet · built this hackathon
