Why Pedalshield exists

Your fitness app is a surveillance device with a leaderboard.

Heatmaps have exposed military bases, stalking victims, and home addresses. The deal was rewards for data. We think that deal is robbery — so we built the opposite.

⛨

Your route is never uploaded

Not stored on our servers, not "anonymized", not encrypted-and-uploaded. It never leaves the device. There is no route database to breach or subpoena.

⚙

Enforced by code, not a policy

The payload that leaves your phone is pinned by an open-source unit test that rejects location, motion, and sensor fields. You can read it and run it yourself.

🔑

Your keys, your money

Payouts go straight to a shielded Zcash address only you control. We can't freeze it, take it back, or see what you do with it.

Don't trust us — read the code on GitHub. The privacy contract is MIT-licensed and independently verifiable.

Real rides only

The treasury pays cyclists — not cars, couches, or GPS spoofs.

Every ride is scored on your phone by a layered anti-cheat engine before a single zatoshi moves. That's what keeps rewards flowing to people who actually ride.

✓ Catches cars

Speed envelope, missing road-buzz vibration, and no pedaling cadence in the motion data — a car ride fails on all three.

✓ Catches fake GPS

Teleport jumps, impossibly constant speeds, and synthetic ruler-straight tracks are hard-failed before payout.

✓ Catches walks

Real cyclists break ~15 km/h at least briefly. A walk dressed up as a ride never does — and never gets paid.

Honest limits: the anti-cheat is layered, not perfect, and we publish what it doesn't catch. ZK route proofs and a community-signed treasury are on the roadmap, not live today.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

How much do I actually earn?
About $0.09 per mile, paid in shielded ZEC — each mile ridden instead of driven avoids ~1 lb of CO2, and we pay you its value at the EPA's social cost of carbon (~$190/tonne), with a per-ride cap. That's deliberate: rewards come from a finite treasury, not a minted token, so they're sustainable instead of a pump-and-dump. Privacy is the product; the ZEC is a bonus.
Is this another crypto ponzi like StepN?
No. There is no Pedalshield token, nothing to buy, no NFT sneakers, and no yield promises. You install a free app, ride, and receive small amounts of ZEC — an established cryptocurrency — from a capped treasury. If the treasury runs dry, payouts pause; nobody is left holding a bag.
Do I need to know anything about crypto?
You need a Zcash wallet address, which takes about two minutes: install a free wallet like Zodl, copy your address, paste it into Pedalshield once. Done. We never touch your keys.
What data leaves my phone?
A minimal claim: distance, duration, and an integrity score. No coordinates, no route, no accelerometer or sensor data — an open-source unit test pins the exact payload and rejects those fields. No account, no email, no identity required to ride.
I'm on Android — can I ride?
iPhone first; Android is next. Drop your email in the box below and you'll be the first to know when it lands.
Will it drain my battery?
Tracking runs only during a ride you start and stop yourself — comparable to any cycling app for the duration of your ride, and nothing in the background the rest of the day.
